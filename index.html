We Capture Your Special Moments

SR Frames Digital Studio, based in Ooruttambalam, captures the connections and emotions you celebrate on your most important days. Our images explore the love, the joy and the togetherness of you, your family and your friends, creating a gallery you will treasure for life.

Tell us your plans and let your day unfold naturally. We believe it is a collaboration, so let us know what makes your celebration special and we will give it back to you as a photograph.
